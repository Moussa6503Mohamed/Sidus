  rubric; atomically writes status, canonical id, and names-only audit.

  Draft/retired questions, draft/stale/foreign rubrics, and replacement attempts fail safely.

  refusal and 5xx sanitization, and forwards raw selection body to Core strict decoder.
- Reviewer/admin UI selects only verified current rubrics before confirmation, repairs historical
  null rows, shows canonical marker, and blocks empty selection/replacement. Editors have no
  selection controls.
- No learner route added. Future learner projection remains required to omit canonical id, rubric,
  and `answerKey`.
